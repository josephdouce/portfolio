Power Platform · 2026

ISP · Integrated Service Platform

One connected platform for a whole field service operation. A customer's request, the quote, the supplier paperwork, the plan, the technician's visit, the signed service report and the invoice all live on the same Dataverse records. It replaced spreadsheets, shared inboxes and a legacy Canvas app.

One request, eight steps, zero re-keying

The customer raises a request on the portal. A manager triages it, and urgent requests get an automatic Teams meeting with the machine's engineers. One click turns it into a quote or a job. AI reads the supplier's replies, the job is planned on a drag-and-drop board, and the technician works through it on their phone. The signed report is emailed to the customer, and billing pulls the order and invoice numbers back from the ERP.

The Service Hub

A Power Apps code app written in React, Vite and TypeScript. It has 24 modules, and every page and control is gated per user. The same app runs on a planner's widescreen and a technician's phone.

In the technician's pocket

A nine-step job wizard: Safety, Details, Hours, Report, Faults, Parts, Attachments, Survey and Review. A risk assessment comes before any work starts. Signatures are captured on the glass, and marking the job complete emails the PDF report to the customer before the engineer has left site.

The Customer Portal

A Power Pages code site in React and Tailwind. Customers see their own machines, track quotes and orders live, upload POs, download signed service reports and raise requests that land directly in the Service Hub. Web roles plus table and column permissions keep each account's data to itself, and cost and supplier columns are never exposed.

Automation & AI

  • Supplier paperwork: suppliers reply to a shared mailbox. AI Builder reads each PDF, classifies it as a quote, order acknowledgement, delivery note or invoice, matches it to the right quote, files it and moves the quote forward.
  • Machine briefing: a Copilot Studio agent grounded in Dataverse summarises a machine's history, recurring faults and open actions.
  • Teams meetings: urgent portal requests create a meeting with the right engineers, and the join link appears on the customer's request.
  • Reports and approvals: completed jobs generate, email and file the service report, and approvals notify the technician automatically.

How it's built

Front endsReact and TypeScript with Vite, Tailwind v4 and shadcn/ui (Radix), TanStack Query and Table, Zustand, React Router, Tiptap and Recharts.
DataOne Dataverse model behind both products, with 40+ typed OData services generated from the schema.
DocumentsjsPDF and html2canvas for reports and estimates, SheetJS for Excel exports, and SharePoint through a Power Automate bridge.
IntegrationsSharePoint, Outlook, Teams, Power BI and the ERP, AI Builder, Copilot Studio and Synthesia.
ALMShips as a managed solution with a scripted DEV → PROD deploy, environment variables and lint gates. One command builds and publishes to the tenant.
SecurityEntra ID sign-in, per-module permissions in the Hub, and server-side web roles and table permissions on the portal.
